18 U.S.C. § 2257 Record-Keeping Requirements Compliance Statement

Last updated: April 2026

goodgurl.gg ("the Site") is a user-generated content platform. All content displayed on this Site is produced, uploaded, and published solely by its registered users. The Site does not itself produce, create, or direct any sexually explicit content.

Primary Producer Exemption

goodgurl.gg is not a "primary producer" as that term is defined under 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75. The Site operates exclusively as a "secondary producer" within the meaning of the statute, in that it merely replicates, transmits, displays, or distributes content submitted by third-party users.

Each user who uploads or publishes sexually explicit content to this Site is solely responsible for complying with all applicable record-keeping and labelling requirements under 18 U.S.C. § 2257 and 28 C.F.R. Part 75 with respect to the content they submit.

Age Verification & Platform Policy

The Site strictly prohibits content depicting individuals under the age of 18. All users must affirm that they are 18 years of age or older at the time of registration. Any content that is reported or found to depict minors will be removed immediately and reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) via the CyberTipline as required by law.

Custodian of Records

Records required to be maintained pursuant to 18 U.S.C. § 2257 are kept by the individual users who produced the content. For inquiries regarding records maintained by the Site operator, please contact:

goodgurl.gg – Compliance Officer
Email: legal@goodgurl.gg
User-Generated Content Disclaimer

All models, actors, actresses, and other persons who appear in any visual depiction of sexually explicit conduct appearing on this website were 18 years of age or older at the time such depictions were created. This representation is made by each individual user who submits such content; goodgurl.gg makes no independent verification and relies entirely on user attestations and platform policies to enforce this requirement.

Reporting Violations

If you believe any content on this Site violates 18 U.S.C. § 2257 or depicts a minor, please contact us immediately at legal@goodgurl.gg or use the in-platform report function. We take all such reports seriously and will act promptly.

This statement is provided for informational and compliance purposes. Nothing herein constitutes legal advice. For questions about your own record-keeping obligations as a content creator, consult a qualified attorney.
